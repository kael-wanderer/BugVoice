BugVoice logo options

Fifteen directions for the app icon and the menu-bar glyph. Each card shows the macOS icon at 200, 64, 32 and 16 px and the monochrome glyph in light and dark menu bars. The colours come from the app mockup: blue accent #2f6df6 and record red #e5484d.

Source: docs/design/logo-options.html. Pick one, and the chosen SVG becomes App/AppIcon.icns and the menu-bar template image.
