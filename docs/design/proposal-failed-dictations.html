Failed dictations

Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-failed-dictations-design.md. A recording whose transcription failed is kept for 24 hours (at most 5) and listed on the Dictation page until it is retried or deleted. Retry copies the text; it never pastes.

1. Dictation page with two failed recordings

The section sits above the stat tiles and appears only when something failed. Each row: time, app, length, and the reason in plain words. Buttons: Retry, Delete.

2. Retry in progress, then done

Retry runs the same speech and cleanup steps with the original app's style. The result goes to the clipboard and into history; the row leaves the list. A second failure keeps the row and updates its reason.
