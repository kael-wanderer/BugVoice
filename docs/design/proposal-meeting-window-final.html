Window

  • Opens on the right third of the screen the meeting started on, full height; you can move or resize it and it remembers.
  • Floats above Meet; clicking Mark, Pause or the tabs does not take focus from Meet. Typing in My notes or Ask does.
  • Collapse leaves only the top bar (Recording, timer).

Ask anything

  • Answers from this meeting's transcript so far plus your notes, with the Meeting notes model.
  • What did I miss? asks for the last 5 minutes.
  • The answer shows above the box: Add to notes, Copy, Close. Asking all past meetings comes with Ask AI (item 6).

After Stop

  • The window stays open while the final transcript and summary are written, then shows the finished meeting.
  • Notes, marks and the summary appear in the meeting's detail page.
