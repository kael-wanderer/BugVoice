BugVoice — release note draft

Markdown · saved to Notes/bugvoice-release-note-draft.md · style: document

BugVoice 0.1 is a local dictation app for macOS. Hold Right Option, speak in English or Vietnamese, and the text appears where your cursor is.

  • Speech-to-text runs on device with WhisperKit.
  • Cleanup uses any local model through an OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible server.

Nothing you say leaves your Mac. Next we will add meeting recording with a searchable timeline

Listening · writes at the cursor · tap ⌥ to stop

Settings

Same five tabs as the Settings mockup below (General, Speech, Text model, Styles, Vocabulary), shown inside the main window. ⌘, opens this page. Sub-project 2 adds a Meetings tab (audio sources, storage folder, auto-delete after N days) and sub-project 3 adds Notes (folder, default style).

Meeting recorder (sub-project 2)

Start from the menu bar, ⌃⌥M, or the Meetings page. A small floating window stays on top during the call: two level meters (you and the call), the latest transcribed line, pause and stop. Stopping saves the recording to Meetings and finishes the transcript in the background.
