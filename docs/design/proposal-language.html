Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-language-and-fix-last-design.md. Set the dictation language per app, with a switch in the menu, or with a key while speaking; fix the last paste with the raw text or the other language.

The existing Language picker becomes the switch's other home. New: the language key, the menu switch toggle, English words in Vietnamese, and the per-app list with the first-launch defaults.

2. Menu bar popover

The language switch sits under the tiles, one click instead of Dictation options › Language (which stays). The Last dictation card gains Raw and the other-language button next to Copy and Retry. While the switch is EN or VI, the menu bar icon carries a badge.
